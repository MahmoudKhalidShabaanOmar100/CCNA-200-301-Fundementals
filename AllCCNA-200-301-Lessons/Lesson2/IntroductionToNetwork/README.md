# 01-Introduction To The Network:

## 01.1-What Is The Computer Network?

  - 01.1.1)**The Computer Network Is A Collection Of Different Devices Like (IP Camera , Personal Computers (PCs) , Laptops , IPads , Smart Phones , Network Printer , Or ETC) That Connected Through Any Type Of Physical Media (Physical Medium) Like Wired Network Connection (Twisted Pair Cable (Electrical Signals) , Or Fiber Optic Cable (Light Pulses)) , Or Wireless Network Connection (WI-FI (Spread Spectrum (Radio Frequencies , Or Radio Waves)) To Share Common Resources , Or Exchange Information...
  - 01.1.2)**The Frist Computer Network Is ARPANet , It Is Started To Work In October 1968 , It Is Between Two US Research Institutes , And Marking The Born Of The Internet...
  - 01.1.3)**ARPANet Transformed To Use TCP/IP In 1983...
  - 01.1.4)**The Main Purpose Of The ARPANet Project Is Mainly Used To Connect Two Mainframes Devices In Two Different States In USA (American United States)...

## 01.3-What Is Different Computer Network Components?

  - 01.3.1)**Client: It Refers To The User That Accesses Service That Made Available By The Server , It Is Also Called Node , Endpoints , Endhosts , Users , Devices Like Personal Computers (PCs) , Laptops , Smart Phones , IPads , And More...
  - 01.3.2)**Network Device: It Is Providing The Easy , And Best Way To Connect Different Clients To The Same Server In The Same Time , It Is Also Called Intermediate Device , Centralized Device Like Switch , Hub , Router , Or ETC...
    - 01.3.2.1)**Switch: It Is Mainly Used To Connect Different Clients To The Same Server , And There Different Characteristics Of The Switch Network Device Like:
      - 01.3.2.1.1)**It Provides Connectivity Between Different Clients In The Same LAN (Local Area Network)...
      - 01.3.2.1.2)**It Does Not Provide Connectivity Between Different LANS (Local Area Network) Over The Internet...
      - 01.3.2.1.3)**It Is Mainly Used To Forward Data (Traffic) From The Source Client To All Destination Clients , Or Only One Destination Client In The Same LAN (Local Area Network)...
    - 01.3.2.2)**Router: It Is Mainly Used To Connect Different LANS (Local Area Networks) Over The Internet , And There Are Different Characteristics Of The Router Network Device Like:
      - 01.3.2.2.1)**It Provides Connectivity Between Different LANS (Local Area Networks Over The Inernet...
      - 01.3.2.2.2)**It Does Not Provide Connectivity Between Different Clients In The Same LAN (Local Area Network)...
      - 01.3.2.2.3)**It Is Mainly Used To Detect Errors , And Determine The Best For The DATA (Traffic) To Take From The Source Client To The Destination Client...
    - 01.3.2.3)**Repeater: It Is Used In The Past With The Coaxial Cable In The Large Distance Up To 100 Meters To Regenerate , And Retransmit The Electrical Signals A New 100 Meters To Reach The DATA (Traffic) From The Source Client To The Destination Client...
    - 01.3.2.4)**Hub: It Is Used To Connect Different Clients To The Same Server , And There Are Different Characteristics Of The Hub Network Device Like:
      - 01.3.2.4.1)**It Provides Connectivity Between Different Clients In The Same LAN (Local Area Network)...
      - 01.3.2.4.2)**It Does Not Provide Connectivity Between Different LANS (Local Area Networks) Over The Internet... 
      - 01.3.2.4.3)**It Is A Stupid Network Device , It Is Mainly Used To Forward Traffic (DATA) From The Source Client To All Different Destination Clients In The Same LAN (Local Area Network)...
    - 01.3.2.5)**Firewall: It Is A Speicality Network Security Device Which Filtering Different DATA (Traffic) Entering , Or Exiting Your LAN (Local Area Network) , And There Are Different Characteristics Of The Network Firewall Like:
      - 01.3.2.5.1)**It Is Mainly Used To Permit , Or Deny Passing Traffic (DATA) According To Different Security Policies...
      - 01.3.2.5.2)**Firewall Can Be Placed Inside LAN (Local Area Network) Before The Router , Or Can Be Placed Outside The LAN (Local Area Network) After The Router...
      - 01.3.2.5.3)**It Is Used To Control , Manage , And Monitor Different Traffics (DATA) That Entering , Or Exiting The LAN (Local Area Network)...
      - 01.3.2.5.4)**The Next Generation Firewall Contains More Advanced , And Modern Filtering Capabilities...
  - 01.3.3)**Server: It Is Mainly Used To Offer Services For Different Clients In The Same LAN (Local Area Network) , Or WAN (Wide Area Network) Like File Sharing Server , Mail Server...
  - 01.3.4)**Physical Media: It Refers To The Way Of Connecting Different Network Components (Clients , Network Devices , And Servers) With Each Others , And It Is Also Called Physical Medium , And There Are Different Types Of Physical Mediums (Physical Medias) Like:
    - 01.3.4.1)**Wired Network Connection: It Is Mainly Connecting Different Network Components (Clients , Network Devices , Servers) By Using Cables For Example:
        - 01.3.4.1.1)**Twisted Pair Cable: Traffic (DATA) Represented Into Electrical Signals...
        - 01.3.4.1.2)**Fiber Optic Cable: Traffic (DATA) Represented Into Light Pulses...
    - 01.3.4.2)**Wireless Network Connection: It Is Mainly Connecting Different Network Components (Clients , Network Devices , Servers) For Example:
        - 01.3.4.2.1)**WI-FI Signals: Traffic (DATA) Represented Into Spread Spectrum (Radio Waves , Or Radio Frequencies)...
    - 01.3.4.3)Physical Media , Or Physical Medium Are Plugged In NIC (Network Interface Card) Into Clients , And Servers...
